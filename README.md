## Technical Checklist for Evaluating Agencies

### 1. Local Market Fit
- Confirm the agency understands each island's search behaviour
- The site describes each island as its own market: Maui visitors book last-minute, Oahu searches happen in English and Japanese, Hawaii Island spans two markets about two hours apart, and Kauai runs on referrals and reviews.

### 2. SEO
- Confirm the scope: Google Business Profile, on-page, technical and local links
- SEO covers Google Business Profile, on-page, technical and local link work, with campaigns tailored to searches in places such as Honolulu, Kahului, Hilo and Lihue. The methods named are data-driven keyword targeting, on-page optimization and ethical link building.

### 3. Web Design
- Confirm mobile-first builds, speed and platform
- Web design means responsive, mobile-first websites built on Avada and custom WordPress, optimized for speed, SEO and user experience, from custom WordPress designs to advanced e-commerce.

### 4. Google Ads
- Confirm campaign types and reporting transparency
- Google Ads management covers search, Local Service Ads and Performance Max campaigns, handled by certified Google Ads and Meta Ads specialists, from keyword bidding to ad creative, with transparent reporting and no markup games.

### 5. Content and Social Media
- Confirm content creation and social media marketing scope
- Content creation includes professional photography and videography and blog copywriting, made to perform across the website, social media and ad campaigns. Social media marketing covers posting, reels and paid social, designed around Hawaii's culture and local audience behaviour across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn or TikTok, with content meant to sound like the client rather than a mainland agency.

### 6. Process and Terms
- Confirm the process, reporting and contract terms
- The process runs in five steps: audit, plan, build, launch and grow. The audit reviews the site, rankings, ads and Google Business Profile against the searches that bring in the most revenue, and the final step is monthly reporting tied to calls, forms and bookings. Engagements are month-to-month with 30-day notice and no annual contracts. The site also says it avoids PBNs, syndicated link networks and AI-only content without senior review.
